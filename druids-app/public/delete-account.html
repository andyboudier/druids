Druids Lodge Polo Club

Delete your account

You can delete your Druids Lodge Polo Club app account, and the information that goes with it, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. On Home, tap Your profile.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete my account, then confirm with your password, or with Google or Apple.

Your sign-in, your profile and picture, and your notification settings are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email abi@druidspolo.co.uk from the address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We will do it within one month and confirm by email.

What is kept

You can delete your account in the app: Home → Your profile → Delete my account. This removes your sign-in, your profile and picture straight away. Your bookings and your player record stay with the club for the times above, without the link to your account; email us to have those deleted too.

See the privacy notice for how long the club keeps each kind of record.
